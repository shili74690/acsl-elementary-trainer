Cheat sheet

Know these cold and the rest is bookkeeping.

Powers of 2: 1 2 4 8 16 32 64 128 256 512 1024
Powers of 8: 1 8 64 512 4096
Powers of 16: 1 16 256 4096
  • Binary is the bridge: 3 bits make an octal digit, 4 bits make a hex digit.
  • To compare numbers in different bases, convert them all to base 10 (or all to hex) first.
  • Carry and borrow at the base: 8 in octal, 16 in hex. Turn letters into values before you add.
  • To count 1s, expand every digit into its 3 or 4 bits. Ignore leading zeros when counting 0s.
  • In #RRGGBB, a pair such as A8 is 10 × 16 + 8 = 168.
